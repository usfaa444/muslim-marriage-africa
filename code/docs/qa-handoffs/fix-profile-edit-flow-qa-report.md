- **Pixel comparison with the Stitch captures:** the CDN and fonts are blocked, so the comparison is structural (layout, copy, order, widths). Slot heights on `/pin` depend on the Tailwind version (`h-13`).
- **Real back/forward cache restore:** not reproducible in headless Chromium here (A2.6); covered by simulated `pageshow` and by real Back navigation.
- **WebKit and Lighthouse:** neither is configured in this repo; vitals were measured with Playwright under emulated 3G and 4x CPU.
- **Photo blur seen by another member:** needs a published profile with an approved photo, which needs the moderation and publication pipeline. Verified instead that the diff touches no media, blur or browse file, that tokens are owner-bound and expire, and that the owner view is unchanged. The live photo case was made by setting one asset to `live` in the database.
- **Staging:** not used.
