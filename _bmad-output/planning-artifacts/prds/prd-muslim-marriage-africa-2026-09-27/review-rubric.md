# PRD Quality Review — muslim-marriage-africa

## Overall verdict
This PRD holds up as a real decision record: a Burkina-specific thesis, evidence-labeled Farata contrast, named-protagonist journeys that exercise FR IDs, and NFRs with numeric bounds rather than adjectives. What is at risk is not the strategy but a handful of load-bearing mechanics — who marks a **meeting**, who resumes a Mahram pause, when phone/WhatsApp may pass, and what Premium actually includes in MVP — that UX, architecture, and stories will otherwise invent. Legal and ops gates are tagged honestly; they are not yet owned or sized, so a green-light would still be a leap.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
Hard product bets are written as bets, not “considerations.” §1 states the thesis in one line: “safety and dignity are a right; reach is a product.” A1/A2/A3 name the age-gate, Mahram-proof, and hosting choices and what was refused (kinship papers; pretending a region pick). Addendum §2 records rejected alternatives (all-or-nothing Farata blur; paywalled Sister chat; race to 0 FCFA). Open Questions in §16 are actually open — fail-closed tolerance, USSD cost, which scholars lend names — not rhetorical.

A decision-maker pushing back would still find three objections only half-acknowledged. First, FR-012 (“Every new Profile is human-reviewed before public visibility”) plus Fail-closed (FR-067) plus a 24h report SLA (FR-083) is an ops company, not a feature; UJ-4 calls T&S a “small team,” and FR-013’s 24h review target is “until ops measures real BF capacity,” but no fallback if the queue cannot hold. Second, §2.2 and §13 make first-wife awareness a non-goal “unless she consents,” while UJ-2 celebrates Ibrahim “already married, seeking a second wife with honesty” — honesty toward the Sister (FR-037), not toward the existing wife. That given-up is not named, and it collides with the Vision that “families… will treat the product as a known honorable path.” Third, Premium is described as “higher Invite quotas, ranking, convenience” (§8, FR-110) while D37’s ranking preference “Ships with boosts” (NEXT, FR-111) and the Premium Invite quota is only “published on the pricing page” (FR-044) — a buyer cannot see what they are approving.

`[NOTE FOR PM]` appears once, at §14.2 deferrals (iOS, carousel, D9, dashboard). Real tensions live in Open Questions and legal flags instead. That is usable; it is not the same as putting the ops and first-wife fights on the decision page.

### Findings
- **medium** Human-review-everything is unsized (§4.1 FR-012, FR-013; §5 NFR-003; UJ-4) — The PRD locks “human-reviewed before public visibility,” Fail-closed holds, and “Report first human decision p95 ≤ 24h” without a staffing model or a degrade path if BF ops cannot meet the clocks. FR-013 already admits the 24h figure is a working target. *Fix:* Add a `[NOTE FOR PM]` that states the launch staffing floor (or a hard cap on public listings) and the product behavior when the free-review or report SLA is breached beyond “flagged as SLA-breach.”
- **medium** First-wife non-goal does not name the given-up (§2.2, §13, Open Question 1, UJ-2) — “First wives who have not consented to awareness” are Non-Users; Ibrahim’s path is in scope. The objection “this is not honorable if the first wife does not know” is not acknowledged as a trade-off. *Fix:* One sentence in §13 or A2’s neighborhood: what was given up (first-wife notice) and why (doxxing / consent / scope), plus the mosque-rumor control that must carry that choice.
- **medium** Premium MVP contents and ranking horizon disagree (§8, FR-044, FR-110, Appendix A D37) — FR-110 says Premium differences are “Invites, ranking, convenience”; D37 says ranking preference ships with NEXT boosts; Premium daily quota has no number. *Fix:* List the MVP Premium delta as an enumerated, testable set (quota N, any ranking rule, any queue perk) and move ranking to NEXT if it only ships with FR-111.

## Substance over theater — strong
The Vision cannot be swapped into a generic matrimony PRD: Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso, Orange Money, Blur, a father who reads the Chat, “Verified marriages — starting at zero.” Farata is cited only with the three evidence labels from `docs/competitor-farata.md`; “+247.8k actifs” vs Play 10k+ is used to forbid inventing counts (SM-C1), not to decorate a Competitive Landscape section. Six journeys look like a lot of personas; each one drives a locked rule (Fatim → D8 Blur; Ibrahim → D14 + XOF rails; Ousmane → D1 read-all; Aïcha → audited unblur; Aminata/Yusuf → dual-confirm; Kadiatou → CIL + proof-backed metrics). Addendum §3 holds the job-map depth so §2.1 stays a JTBD list, not a persona novella. NFRs in §5 have targets (text p95 < 10s; Lite grid ≤ 8s; erasure 30 days) and verification methods — not “the system must be scalable / secure / reliable.”

No findings. Furniture that exists (name shortlist, aesthetic hint) is labeled as not-this-document.

## Strategic coherence — strong
The thesis in §1 is load-bearing: free safety and Sister dignity, Brothers pay for reach, north star is “chaperoned meetings plus dual-confirmed nikah — not DAU.” Feature grouping follows that arc (paywall bans on Verification/Blur/Mahram/Report; Fail-closed; honest counter at 0). MVP kind is a full launch platform, not a thin problem-solving slice — 74 unique P/D IDs at earliest-horizon MVP, all six `system-idea.md` must-haves in §6 — but that matches “become the honorable local reference **before** ‘another dating app from Dakar’ owns the mental model” (§9), not a random backlog. Success Metrics validate the thesis (SM-1 marriages, SM-2 meetings, SM-5 Blur/Mahram/Sister-initiated Invites) and name counter-metrics (SM-C1–C4) that forbid the obvious Goodharts.

The weak joint is SM-2: it “Validates FR-028” via a **meeting** stage flag whose actor is unspecified (see Done-ness). Dual-confirm (SM-1) is fully specified; half the stated north star is not. That does not collapse the thesis, but it means launch could optimize the one measurable half (marriage reports) and leave chaperoned meetings as a label.

### Findings
- **medium** Half the north star has no implementable event (§15 SM-2; FR-028; FR-082) — SM-2 counts “Chaperoned / family meetings marked” while FR-082 is NEXT and FR-028 only says stage is **chat** “until a meeting is marked.” Without a who/when, SM-2 cannot validate the thesis. *Fix:* Either define the MVP mark (who may set **meeting**, what evidence, Mahram visibility) or drop meetings from the north star until D3 ships and keep SM-1 as the sole primary.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate
Almost every FR has Given/When/Then criteria, horizons are marked, and NFRs state bounds instead of “reasonable performance.” An engineer can implement the majority of FR-001–FR-143 without inventing the product. The failures cluster in the core path, which is where this dimension must be unforgiving.

FR-028/FR-082 leave “a meeting is marked” without an actor, so SM-2 and the **meeting** stage are not testable. FR-075’s acceptance line names “the Sister or a Moderator” as the resume parties and, in the same sentence, assumes “Sister or Mahram can resume” — three roles, two rules. FR-068’s prose gate is “before mutual consent + Mahram presence”; its criterion is “before mutual contact-share + optional Mahram presence.” Those are different products. FR-021 promises fields “render in French-first copy using Glossary marital-status values,” but §3 never lists those values; FR-024 adds undefined “life plans” and “Distance is included” with no unit or radius. FR-088’s title is “Device / phone / ID linkage”; the criteria only hold ID-hash and phone-OTP reuse. FR-066 lists outcome “blur-and-warn” with no meaning for text vs Photo.

### Findings
- **high** Meeting stage has no actor (§4.2 FR-028; §4.7 FR-082; §15 SM-2) — FR-028: “stage is **chat** until a meeting is marked.” FR-082: “When they mark stage **meeting**, Then it is a stage flag without the full planner.” “They” is unbound. *Fix:* Add MVP criteria: which role(s) may set **meeting**, whether both sides (and Mahram) must agree, and what the UI shows; or remove **meeting** from MVP FR-028 and SM-2.
- **high** Mahram pause-resume contradicts itself (§4.7 FR-075) — Criterion: “compose is disabled until the Sister or a Moderator resumes per published rules `[ASSUMPTION: Sister or Mahram can resume; ended Chats cannot]`.” Stories will fork on whether the Mahram, the Sister, or a Moderator lifts a pause. *Fix:* Pick one resume matrix (and whether ended Chats are terminal) and make the assumption match the Then clause.
- **high** Off-platform gate is untestable (§4.6 FR-068; addendum risk 9) — Body: “before mutual consent + Mahram presence.” Criterion: “before mutual contact-share + optional Mahram presence.” D5/addendum say contact after “mutual + optional Mahram.” QA cannot know whether a WhatsApp handle after mutual accept without a Mahram is allow, hold, or block. *Fix:* State the single predicate (e.g. block until both Members opt into contact-share, Mahram optional vs required) and give one positive and one negative example.
- **medium** Profile and filter enums are missing (§3 Glossary; FR-021; FR-024; FR-037) — FR-021 cites “Glossary marital-status values” that do not exist. FR-024 filters on “life plans” and “Distance” with no definition. FR-037 requires Brothers to declare “single / married” only, which may silently drop divorced/widowed. *Fix:* Put marital-status, polygamy-intent, practice, and life-plans enums in the Glossary and list which FR-021 fields are Invite-ready required.
- **medium** Device fingerprint is named but not accepted (§4.8 FR-088; UJ-4 step 1) — Description: “Device / phone / ID linkage.” Criteria hold “Banned ID document hash” and “same phone OTP.” Device reuse after Ban is the evasion path UJ-4’s “fingerprint hints” imply. *Fix:* Add a device-linkage criterion or strike “Device” from the FR until architecture can honor it.
- **medium** “blur-and-warn” has no consequence (§4.6 FR-066) — Outcomes are “block / blur-and-warn / hold.” Block and hold are testable; blur-and-warn is not defined for text or Voice notes. *Fix:* Define the Member-visible result per modality or drop the outcome from MVP.

## Scope honesty — strong
Omissions do real work. §13 Non-Goals names iOS-in-MVP, live A/V, kinship papers, invented counts, paywalled safety, silent auto-renew, vendor picks, and “Starting UX, architecture, or epics.” Appendix A keeps every P1–P59 and D1–D40 id with a horizon and a deferral reason. `[ASSUMPTION]` tags are dense because this is a Fast-path draft, and §17 indexes them. Open Questions are not disguised decisions. Addendum §2 is the rejected-alternative matrix the PRD itself should not bloat.

Open-item density is high for a document that calls itself “the decision record” for UX, architecture, and stories: twelve Open Questions, ~25 indexed assumptions, three legal flags (A1–A3), one `[NOTE FOR PM]`. Status is still `draft`, so this is honest Fast-path behavior rather than a silent de-scope. It becomes a blocker the moment someone treats this file as green-light-to-build: A1 age law, A3 CIL/hosting, NFR-008 retention, FR-116 “do not ship a fictional board,” and unlocked XOF prices have no owner or revisit condition.

### Findings
- **medium** Launch-blocking opens have no owner (§16 items 1, 4, 5, 12; A1, A3; §8 price; FR-116) — Counsel on 19+, CIL filing, retention clocks, named scholars, and price lock are launch gates. *Fix:* On each, add owner + revisit condition (e.g. “before UX lock” vs “before Play submit”) or move them to a phase-blocker list so downstream does not start on a pretend-closed PRD.

## Downstream usability — adequate
This is a chain-top PRD (UX → architecture → stories). It is mostly source-extractable: §3 Glossary, contiguous FR-001–FR-143, NFR-001–NFR-009, SM-1–SM-6 plus SM-C1–C4, UJ-1–UJ-6 each with a named protagonist and inline context. Cross-references generally resolve. Sections can be pulled with Glossary nouns rather than “see above.”

What will fork downstream is vocabulary that the Glossary promised to stabilize and did not. “Ice Breaker” is a product noun (FR-047) but not a Glossary term. FR-015 shows a “Mahram-verified level”; FR-078 and A2 award a “verified wali” badge — §3 says requirements use **Mahram**, member-facing copy may say *wali*. “contact-share” (FR-068) and “life plans” (FR-024) are undefined. FR-116 points at “FR-123-board,” which is not an FR. UJ-3’s `[ASSUMPTION]` that “Mahram accounts cannot send Invites or appear in the grid” is not in §17 (the index lists journey *names*, not that rule). Combined with the FR-075/FR-068 contradictions above, story writers will not share one extract.

### Findings
- **medium** Glossary does not cover nouns the FRs treat as canonical (§3; FR-015; FR-024; FR-047; FR-068; FR-078) — Downstream was told “FRs, UJs, and SMs use them verbatim.” They do not. *Fix:* Add Ice Breaker, contact-share, life plans, and one badge name (Mahram-verified **or** verified-wali); align FR-015 and FR-078 to that name.

## Shape fit — strong
Consumer, multi-stakeholder, religiously and legally constrained, chain-top: user journeys with named protagonists are load-bearing and present; CIL/age/retention are traced to FRs and NFRs rather than a decorative Compliance heading; technical-how is correctly parked in the addendum. Six UJs match six jobs (Sister, Brother, Mahram, Moderator, couple, Operator) — not journey-theater on a single-operator tool, and not a capability spec pretending to be a consumer product. Brownfield references are absent because this is greenfield; Farata is competitor evidence, not an existing-code baseline. The document is long because the concerns are real (minors, haya, payments, moderation, CIL), not because a template was filled. Name, visual system, and wrapper technology are correctly refused as this PRD’s job.

No findings.
